# Review manuscript prompt

Choose internal-audit or blind-referee mode and read the complete frozen manuscript, including every main proof, claim map, source records and experiment artifacts.

Audit statement/proof/assumption/dependency correctness, endpoints, asymptotics, numerics, citations, novelty positioning, reproducibility and abstract/body consistency. Attempt simple counterexamples where appropriate.

Return FATAL, MAJOR, MINOR and STRENGTH items. Each item needs issue ID, location, affected claims, failed step, counterexample, required fix and post-fix verification, followed by a conditional recommendation and unread/uncertain material.
