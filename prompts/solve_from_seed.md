Actively test the weakest assumptions and simple counterexamples. Mark every missing step GAP and stop rather than hiding it. Separate symbolic/numerical observations from proof.

Return a PROPOSED proof packet, refutation packet or documented obstruction with artifacts and next action. Never self-verify.
