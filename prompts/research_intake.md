# Research intake prompt

Read AGENTS.md, the quality standard and workflow 00. Do not try to solve the problem.

## Inputs

Problem/topic, domain, available sources, target output, deadline/budget, numerical/formalization permission, privacy/provider constraints.

## Task

Produce precise candidate targets with domains, quantifiers, parameters, regularity, boundary/initial conditions; explicit assumptions and notation; theorem/conjecture/question/empirical classes; Definition of Done; initial risks, ambiguities and human decisions.

## Output

Update only the intake state files. Mark missing information OPEN GAP/UNKNOWN. Do not create a VERIFIED fact or invent a source.
